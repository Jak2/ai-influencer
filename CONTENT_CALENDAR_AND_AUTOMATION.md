# 1. HOW TO CREATE A CONTENT CALENDAR

## What Is a Content Calendar?

A content calendar is a schedule that maps out **what** you post, **when** you post it, **where** you post it, and **why**. It eliminates daily decision-making and ensures consistency.

## The 5-Step Calendar Creation Process

### Step 1: Define Your Content Pillars (30 min, one time)

Content pillars are the 3-5 recurring themes your character posts about. Everything you create falls into one of these buckets.

```
EXAMPLE FOR FITNESS AI INFLUENCER:

Pillar 1: WORKOUTS (30% of content)
  - Gym selfies, form tips, workout clips, before/after

Pillar 2: LIFESTYLE (25% of content)
  - Morning routine, meals, coffee, day-in-life

Pillar 3: MOTIVATION (20% of content)
  - Quotes, pep talks, progress updates, mindset

Pillar 4: PERSONALITY/FUN (15% of content)
  - Memes, trends, relatable humor, Q&A

Pillar 5: PROMOTION (10% of content)
  - Product links, subscription plugs, brand deals, digital products
```

### Step 2: Choose Your Posting Frequency

```
MINIMUM VIABLE POSTING (for one person doing everything):

Instagram:
  - Feed posts: 1/day (7/week)
  - Stories: 5-10/day
  - Reels: 3-5/week

TikTok:
  - Videos: 1-3/day (7-21/week)

Twitter/X:
  - Tweets: 3-5/day (21-35/week)

YouTube:
  - Shorts: 3-5/week
  - Long-form: 1/week (optional, start later)

TOTAL WEEKLY CONTENT PIECES:
  - Feed images: 7
  - Reels/TikToks: 5-10 (cross-post same video)
  - Stories: 35-70
  - Tweets: 21-35
```

### Step 3: Assign Themes to Days

```
WEEKLY THEME MAP:

MONDAY - "Motivation Monday"
  Pillar: Motivation
  Feed: Motivational quote + gym photo
  Reel: "Start your week right" clip
  Stories: Weekly goals, poll ("What are you working on?")
  Tweets: 3 motivational tweets

TUESDAY - "Tip Tuesday"
  Pillar: Workouts
  Feed: Exercise form tip / tutorial
  Reel: Quick workout demo
  Stories: "Try this" tutorial slides
  Tweets: Fitness tips thread

WEDNESDAY - "Wellness Wednesday"
  Pillar: Lifestyle
  Feed: Meal prep / healthy recipe / self-care
  Reel: "What I eat in a day" montage
  Stories: Recipe slides, food polls
  Tweets: Wellness advice, questions

THURSDAY - "Throwback / Thirst Trap Thursday"
  Pillar: Personality
  Feed: Best photo of the week (highest engagement day for attractive content)
  Reel: Outfit transition or "get ready with me"
  Stories: "Which outfit?" polls, Q&A
  Tweets: Fun/flirty tweets

FRIDAY - "Fun Friday"
  Pillar: Personality/Fun
  Feed: Weekend vibes, going out prep
  Reel: Trending audio + your niche twist
  Stories: Weekend plans, "this or that"
  Tweets: Relatable tweets, memes

SATURDAY - "Spotlight Saturday"
  Pillar: Promotion (soft sell)
  Feed: Lifestyle photo with subtle product placement
  Reel: Day-in-life vlog style
  Stories: Behind-the-scenes, exclusive sneak peeks
  Tweets: Engagement tweets, shoutouts

SUNDAY - "Self-care Sunday"
  Pillar: Lifestyle + Motivation
  Feed: Cozy/rest day content
  Reel: Weekly recap or reflection
  Stories: Rest day routine, next week preview
  Tweets: Reflective, soft content
```

### Step 4: Build the Calendar in a Tool

Choose one of these (detailed comparison in Section 3):

```
FREE OPTIONS:
- Google Sheets (most flexible, fully customizable)
- Notion (great templates, visual calendar view)
- Trello (kanban-style, drag and drop)
- Google Calendar (simple, date-based)

PAID OPTIONS:
- Later ($25/mo - visual drag-and-drop, auto-publish)
- Buffer ($6/mo - simple multi-platform scheduling)
- Hootsuite ($99/mo - enterprise grade)
- Sprout Social ($249/mo - agency level)
```

### Step 5: Batch Create and Fill the Calendar

```
WEEKLY BATCHING SESSION (2-3 hours, once per week):

Hour 1: IMAGE GENERATION
  - Generate all 7 feed images for the week
  - Generate story graphics/templates
  - Upscale and edit selections

Hour 2: VIDEO CREATION
  - Create 5-10 reels/TikToks for the week
  - Add captions, music, effects in CapCut
  - Export in correct dimensions

Hour 3: COPY & SCHEDULING
  - Write all 7 captions (or use ChatGPT with character bible)
  - Write tweet threads/content
  - Upload everything to your scheduling tool
  - Set publish times
  - Prepare story content

DONE: Entire week scheduled in one session.
```

# 5. PLATFORM-BY-PLATFORM AUTOMATION

## Instagram Automation

### What CAN Be Automated
```
YES - Schedule and auto-publish:
  - Feed posts (photos, carousels)
  - Reels
  - Stories (with some tools)

YES - Automate partially:
  - DM auto-replies (Instagram's built-in auto-responses)
  - Comment responses (saved replies)
  - Hashtag research (tools generate suggestions)
  - Analytics reporting (auto-generated)

TOOLS FOR IG AUTOMATION:
- Meta Business Suite (free, official): Schedule feed + stories + reels
- Buffer / Later (schedule + analytics)
- ManyChat ($15/mo): DM automation, keyword triggers, funnels
- Make.com: Auto-post from Google Drive, auto-reply triggers
```

### What CANNOT Be Automated (Platform Rules)
```
NO - Will get you banned:
  - Auto-following/unfollowing
  - Auto-liking at scale
  - Auto-commenting on others' posts
  - Buying followers or engagement
  - Using unofficial API bots for mass actions

DO MANUALLY:
  - Genuine engagement (commenting on others' posts)
  - DM conversations (beyond auto-reply)
  - Story interactions
  - Community management
```

### Instagram Auto-Post Setup (Free)

```
METHOD 1: Meta Business Suite (100% Free)

1. Go to business.facebook.com
2. Link your Instagram creator/business account
3. Go to "Planner" tab
4. Click "Create Post"
5. Select Instagram
6. Upload image/video, write caption, add hashtags
7. Click "Schedule" and pick date/time
8. Done - it auto-publishes at that time

SUPPORTS:
- Feed posts (photos + carousels)
- Reels
- Stories
- Cross-posting to Facebook simultaneously
- Bulk scheduling (up to 6 months ahead)

LIMITATIONS:
- Interface is clunky
- No visual grid preview
- Basic analytics only
```

# 6. FULL AUTOMATION WORKFLOWS

## Workflow 1: Content Creation to Multi-Platform Posting (Make.com)

```
TRIGGER: New image added to Google Drive "Ready to Post" folder

STEP 1: Make.com detects new file
STEP 2: Pulls the matching caption from a Google Sheet
STEP 3: Resizes image for each platform:
  - 1080x1350 for Instagram feed
  - 1080x1920 for Stories/Reels/TikTok
  - 1200x675 for Twitter
STEP 4: Posts to Instagram via Meta API
STEP 5: Posts to Twitter via X API
STEP 6: Posts to Facebook via Meta API
STEP 7: Sends you a Telegram/Discord notification: "Posted successfully"
STEP 8: Logs the post in a Google Sheet tracker

RESULT: Upload one image → it goes everywhere automatically
COST: Free (within Make.com free tier if <1,000 operations/month)
```

## Workflow 2: DM Funnel Automation (ManyChat + ChatGPT)

```
TRIGGER: Someone DMs you a keyword on Instagram (e.g., "WORKOUT")

STEP 1: ManyChat detects the keyword
STEP 2: Auto-sends: "Hey! Here's your free workout plan [link]"
STEP 3: Waits 24 hours
STEP 4: Auto-sends: "Did you try the plan? I'd love to hear how it went!"
STEP 5: Waits 48 hours
STEP 6: Auto-sends: "Want more exclusive content? Check out my VIP [Fanvue link]"

RESULT: Automated lead nurturing funnel
COST: ManyChat free tier (up to 1,000 contacts) or $15/mo for more
```

## Workflow 3: Auto-Repurpose Content (Make.com)

```
TRIGGER: New Instagram post published

STEP 1: Make.com detects the new post
STEP 2: Downloads the image
STEP 3: Sends image to Canva API → creates Twitter-optimized version
STEP 4: Sends caption to ChatGPT API → rewrites for Twitter tone (shorter, punchier)
STEP 5: Posts to Twitter with new caption
STEP 6: Creates a Pinterest pin with the same image
STEP 7: Schedules a "reminder" repost for 30 days later

RESULT: One Instagram post automatically becomes 3+ pieces of content
COST: Make.com free tier + ChatGPT API ($0.01-0.05 per rewrite)
```

## Workflow 4: Weekly Analytics Auto-Report (Zapier)

```
TRIGGER: Every Monday at 9am

STEP 1: Pull Instagram insights via Meta API
STEP 2: Pull TikTok analytics via API
STEP 3: Pull Twitter analytics via API
STEP 4: Compile into Google Sheets dashboard
STEP 5: Calculate week-over-week growth
STEP 6: Send summary to your email/Telegram

RESULT: Automated weekly performance report
COST: Zapier free tier (if under 100 tasks/month)
```

## Workflow 5: Content Idea Generator (Daily, Automated)

```
TRIGGER: Every morning at 7am

STEP 1: Make.com pulls trending hashtags from Instagram
STEP 2: Pulls trending sounds from TikTok (via scraper or API)
STEP 3: Sends trends to ChatGPT API with prompt:
        "Based on these trends, suggest 5 content ideas for
        [character name], a [niche] AI influencer"
STEP 4: Sends suggestions to your Telegram/Slack/Email

RESULT: Wake up to 5 fresh content ideas every morning
COST: Make.com free + ChatGPT API (~$0.05/day)
```

# 9. STEP-BY-STEP SETUP GUIDES

## Setup Guide A: Buffer Free (Recommended Starter)

```
1. Go to buffer.com → Sign up free
2. Connect your accounts:
   - Click "Channels" → "Connect Channel"
   - Connect Instagram (needs business/creator account)
   - Connect TikTok
   - Connect Twitter/X
3. Set your posting schedule:
   - Click on each channel → "Posting Schedule"
   - Set times (e.g., IG: 9am, 12pm, 6pm)
   - Buffer will auto-publish at these times
4. Create your first post:
   - Click "Create" → "New Post"
   - Select platforms
   - Upload image/video
   - Write caption
   - Click "Add to Queue" or "Schedule"
5. View your calendar:
   - Click "Publishing" → see all scheduled posts
   - Drag and drop to reorder

FREE LIMITS:
  - 3 channels total
  - 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time
  - Basic analytics
```

## Setup Guide B: Meta Business Suite (Free, IG + FB)

```
1. Convert Instagram to Business/Creator account:
   - IG Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account
2. Go to business.facebook.com
   - Create a Meta Business account if you don't have one
   - Link your Instagram account
3. Navigate to "Planner":
   - This is your content calendar
   - See monthly/weekly view of scheduled posts
4. Schedule a post:
   - Click "Create Post" (top right)
   - Select "Instagram Feed" (or Reel, Story)
   - Upload media
   - Write caption + hashtags
   - Click "Schedule" → pick date and time
   - Click "Schedule Post"
5. Schedule Stories:
   - Same process, select "Instagram Story"
   - Add stickers/text in the editor
6. View insights:
   - Go to "Insights" tab
   - See reach, engagement, follower growth

NO LIMITS - completely free, unlimited scheduling
```

## Setup Guide C: Make.com Cross-Post Automation

```
1. Sign up at make.com (free)
2. Create a new Scenario
3. Click "+" to add first module:
   - Search "Google Drive" → "Watch Files"
   - Connect your Google Drive
   - Set folder: "Ready to Post"
4. Add module: "Google Sheets"
   - "Search Rows" → find matching caption by filename
5. Add module: "Instagram"
   - "Create a Photo Post"
   - Map: Image from Google Drive, Caption from Google Sheet
6. Add module: "Twitter"
   - "Create a Tweet"
   - Map: Same image, shorter caption
7. Add module: "Google Sheets"
   - "Update a Row" → Mark as "Posted"
8. Turn on the scenario
9. Set schedule: Check every 15 minutes

RESULT:
Drop image in Google Drive folder → auto-posts to IG + Twitter + updates tracker
```
